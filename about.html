About CardGallery

Our Story & Mission

Dedicated to documenting, archiving, and showcasing the art and strategy behind custom Magic: The Gathering Commander decks.

Our Mission Statement

At CardGallery, our mission is to elevate trading card deck construction into an interactive, visual art form. We believe a Commander deck is more than just a list of 100 cards—it is an expressive expression of social strategy, card aesthetics, and multiplayer interaction.

We strive to provide players, collectors, and enthusiasts with an elegant digital platform to inspect full decklists card by card, examine oracle texts and current market values, and appreciate the careful curation required to balance a five-color political engine.

Our Brand Story

CardGallery began around a cozy kitchen table in Charlotte, North Carolina. Frustrated by static, spreadsheet-like deck displays that failed to convey the social atmosphere of casual tabletop play, our founder set out to build an experiential storefront and gallery dedicated to specific, hand-crafted decks.

Our flagship collection, "Kenrith's Group Hug", embodies this philosophy. Built around the five-color monarch Kenrith, the Returned King, the deck turns the typical adversarial game of Commander into a high-stakes diplomatic negotiation filled with shared bounties, tempting offers, and sudden tactical reversals.

Today, CardGallery serves as the permanent digital home for this collection, bridging the gap between physical card play and interactive web design.
